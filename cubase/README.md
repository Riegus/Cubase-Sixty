# Cubase-Sixty

The SSL **UF8**, **UF1** and **UC1** in Cubase, without SSL 360: the mixer on the faders, V-pots and displays, and
the SSL plug-ins of your tracks on the knobs. A port of [Rea-Sixty](../README.md) (the same controllers in REAPER)
to Cubase's MIDI Remote API, for Windows. Any one of the three surfaces is enough.

- **Using it:** the [user guide](docs/user-guide.md) (also installed as HTML, opened from the tray menu).
- **Installing:** `CubaseSixty-Setup-<version>.exe` from the releases. What it changes on your PC is listed in the
  guide, [What the installer changes on your PC](docs/user-guide.md#what-the-installer-changes-on-your-pc), and
  shown by the installer before anything is installed.
- **Building:** [bridge/README.md](bridge/README.md), [installer/README.md](installer/README.md).
- **What is missing and why:** [known issues](docs/known-issues.md), [Cubase limitations](docs/cubase-limitations.md),
  [missing features](docs/missing-features.md).
