For your user account only (no administrator rights needed):
  - The program in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Cubase-Sixty. Your settings, favourites and logs are kept there too.
  - Four MIDI Remote scripts in Documents\Steinberg\Cubase\MIDI Remote\Driver Scripts\Local\CubaseSixty.
  - If you tick "Start with Windows": an entry in your Run key (HKCU\...\CurrentVersion\Run).
  - While it runs, Cubase-Sixty makes its own virtual MIDI ports (through the teVirtualMIDI driver of loopMIDI or
    SSL 360), and closes SSL 360's background program SSL360Core.exe whenever it starts.
  The uninstaller removes all of this (it asks before deleting your settings and favourites).

Only if you tick "Install the USB driver" (asks for administrator permission once):
  - The SSL controllers are moved from SSL's own USB driver to Windows' WinUSB driver. From then on SSL 360 does
    not see them. Rea-Sixty uses the same driver.
  - The driver package is signed on your PC with a certificate made there ("Rea-Sixty Driver Signer"), which is
    added to the computer's trusted root and trusted publisher certificates. Its private key stays in the
    computer's certificate store.
  The uninstaller does NOT remove the driver (Rea-Sixty may be using it). To undo it: tray menu > USB driver >
  Remove it, before uninstalling. That removes the driver and the certificate; then unplug and replug the
  controllers (if SSL 360 still does not see them, run SSL 360's installer again).

Before installing the driver, it is sensible to have a recent Windows restore point or backup.
